Changelog

v2.1
2026-05-29 ▼
  • Bitcoin Currency Support: Added Bitcoin (BTC) as a display currency option, complete with live exchange rates and high-precision decimal formatting.
  • Thousands Separator: Large monetary and numeric values now display commas for much easier readability.
  • Improved Time Metrics: The "Time Spent" metric card now displays elapsed days when applicable.
  • 12/24 Hour Format & Hover Tooltips: Added configurable 12-hour and 24-hour time formats, and hovering over a study's date now reveals the exact full timestamp.
  • Redesigned Manual Entry: Upgraded the manual entry form with a custom Material 3-inspired date/time picker, and fixed the bonus currency label to correctly match the selected currency.
  • Refined Chart Visuals: Corrected chart bar rounding so that bars are flat on the bottom and rounded on top.
  • Accent Color Picker Fix: Fixed a layout issue where the rainbow icon for custom accent color selection was getting clipped.
v2.0
2026-05-24 ▼
  • CloudResearch CSV Support: Import CloudResearch CSV files! No longer only supports Prolific.
  • Redesigned UI: Completely overhauled UI with the addition of Light/Dark Mode, Accent Colors, better use of widescreen, and more.
  • Stacked Earnings Chart: Earnings chart now displays Pending (Awaiting Review) surveys stacked in a dynamic secondary color on top of Approved.
  • Custom Minimum Wage Configuration: Directly configure your minimum wage in your local currency. Study rate badges color coding now based on your minimum wage.
  • Status Totals: Added total status counts (Approved, Awaiting Review, Screened Out, Rejected, etc.) to the dashboard..
